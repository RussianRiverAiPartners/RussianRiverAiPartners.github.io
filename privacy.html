Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Russian River AI Partners ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect on russianriveraipartners.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Newsletter signup: if you subscribe to our quarterly marketing report, we collect your email address through our email service provider, MailerLite.
  • Chat widget: if you use the chat assistant on our site, the messages you send are processed to generate a response. We don't use chat content for marketing or share it with third parties beyond the service that powers the assistant.
  • Consultation requests: if you book a free consultation, the information you provide (name, email, business details) is used only to prepare for and conduct that consultation.

How we use your information

We use the information above to send the newsletter you signed up for, respond to chat questions, and follow up on consultation requests. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email we send. To have your information deleted or to ask a question about this policy, email us at info@russianriveraipartners.com.

California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and to request its deletion. Contact us at the email above to make a request.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@russianriveraipartners.com.
